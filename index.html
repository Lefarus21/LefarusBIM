                    Frequently Asked Questions & Instructions
                


                

                    

                        
Where do I find my Machine Hash?

                        
When running any Lefarus Detailing command in AutoCAD without an active license, a security dialog appears titled "Lefarus Detailing - License Activation". Click "OK" to automatically copy your 64-character fingerprint to your clipboard.

                    


                    

                        
What happens after I share my hash?

                        
The website manager (Surafel Seyoum Demeke) will either add your workstation hash to the built-in authorized whitelist or provide you with an RSA-signed license file (lefarusBIM.lic) that activates instantly.

                    


                    

                        
Where should the license file be placed?

                        
If you receive a lefarusBIM.lic file, copy it directly into your plugin folder:
                        
C:\ProgramData\Autodesk\ApplicationPlugins\LefarusDetailing.bundle\Contents\

                    


                    

                        
Does changing my network or Wi-Fi break the hash?

                        
No. The Lefarus Detailing multi-factor hardware fingerprinting relies primarily on Motherboard, BIOS UUID, CPU ID, and Disk Serial with OEM placeholder filtering, ensuring stability across standard network and docking station changes.
