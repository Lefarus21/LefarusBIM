Frequently Asked Questions & Instructions

Where do I find my Machine Fingerprint?

When running any Lefarus Detailing command in AutoCAD without an active license, a security dialog appears titled "Lefarus Detailing - License Activation". Click "OK" to automatically copy your 64-character fingerprint to your clipboard.

What happens after I share my fingerprint?

Website Manager Surafel Seyoum Demeke will add your workstation to the whitelist or send you your signed license file (lefarusBIM.lic).

Where do I place my license file?

If you receive a lefarusBIM.lic file, copy it directly into your AutoCAD plugin bundle directory:
C:\ProgramData\Autodesk\ApplicationPlugins\LefarusDetailing.bundle\Contents\

Does changing Wi-Fi or networks invalidate the license?

No. Lefarus Detailing multi-factor hardware fingerprinting locks onto your Motherboard, BIOS UUID, CPU ID, and Disk Serial with OEM placeholder filtering for high stability across normal network changes.
